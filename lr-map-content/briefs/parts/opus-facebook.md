## Goal: a Facebook post and caption

A Facebook image post (feed image or images, your choice of format and size; 1080×1350 portrait is the default for mobile feeds) plus the caption, a first comment, and alt text. Facebook's register is a useful lesson shaped for community conversation: open with a relatable problem or clear lesson, explain briefly with readable spacing, and close with a specific discussion prompt. Start from the source Facebook copy and first comment and improve them. Files: `src/opus-facebook.html`, `copy/opus-facebook.md`.
