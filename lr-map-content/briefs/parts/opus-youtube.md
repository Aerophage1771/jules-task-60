## Goal: a YouTube video storyboard

A production-ready storyboard for a YouTube teaching video on the LR Question Type Map: 16:9 frames (1920×1080) for every scene, each with its voiceover script, on-screen text, timing and edit or motion notes beside it; a thumbnail (1280×720, consider two options); and the upload copy (title, description with chapters that match your timings, pinned comment). YouTube's register sustains explanation and trust: preview, teach, demonstrate, recap. The worked example in `source/alternates.md` (one library argument, six tasks) is the strongest demonstration material in the source. Decide the runtime and scene count. Kit note: an artboard nested inside your own row element stays in place, so you can lay out frame and notes side by side. Files: `src/opus-youtube.html`, `copy/opus-youtube.md` (full script in scene order, plus the upload copy).
